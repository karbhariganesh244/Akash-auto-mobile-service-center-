Yamaha Motorcycles Series

FZ Series

Includes models like the FZ-X, FZ-S FI, and FZ FI, known for their neo-retro design and rugged build.

R Series

Features models like the R15 (V4 and S) and R3, known for their sporty performance and design.

MT Series

Includes the MT-15 V2 and MT-03, known for their aggressive styling and powerful engines.

FZ-X Hybrid

Yamaha's first hybrid motorcycle in the 150cc category.
